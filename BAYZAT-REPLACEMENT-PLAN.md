# Bayzat Replacement Plan

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24

## Scope boundary

This plan targets **publicly documented Bayzat product parity** for hrmny's internal use. It does not copy Bayzat's code, UI, proprietary content, or branding. Exact parity with private, beta, plan-specific, and partner-only features requires access to hrmny's Bayzat tenant and a screen-by-screen export.

Regulated services are integrated through licensed providers rather than rebuilt: insurance brokerage, card issuing, banking, foreign exchange, WPS submission, telemedicine, pharmacy, and mental-health services. Hrmny OS can own the workflow, records, approvals, reporting, and reconciliation around those services.

## Status legend

- **Live foundation**: a production capability already exists and is reused.
- **Building**: durable schema/API/UI work is currently being implemented.
- **Next**: required product work not yet implemented.
- **Provider**: Hrmny OS will orchestrate a licensed or contracted external provider.
- **Tenant audit**: public documentation is insufficient; verify in hrmny's Bayzat account.

## UAE compliance requirements

The replacement must preserve these behaviours in schema, calculations, exports, and acceptance tests:

1. MOHRE identifiers and employment data.
2. WPS-compatible SIF generation and validation; actual submission through a bank or exchange.
3. UAE holiday calendars, workweeks, leave rules, overtime and absence handling.
4. End-of-service gratuity, final settlement, daily wage, leave salary, encashment and air-ticket rules, all versioned by effective date.
5. Passports, visas, Emirates IDs, work permits, trade licences and dependant records with expiry alerts.
6. AED base currency, supported foreign currencies, immutable payroll snapshots and bilingual employee documents.
7. UAE health-policy membership, dependants, endorsements and card records.
8. UAE timezone, least-privilege access, biometric consent/retention controls, audit and documented backup/recovery.

Legal and payroll rules must be approved by hrmny's UAE HR/payroll owner before production calculations are relied upon.

## Cutover gates

Bayzat can be retired only when all applicable gates pass:

- HR signs off employee, document, dependant, asset and policy counts.
- Payroll signs off at least two parallel payroll cycles, WPS/SIF output, payslips, EOS examples and accounting totals.
- Employees complete Dubai-office UAT for self-service, leave, attendance, letters, expenses, payslips and mobile use.
- Security confirms role boundaries, audit coverage, backup restore, data export and leaver revocation.
- Every external provider has a named owner, live credentials, failure/retry procedure and reconciliation report.
- Historical Bayzat exports are stored read-only with an agreed retention period.
- A tested rollback window keeps Bayzat available until final acceptance.

## Information and access needed from hrmny

Development can continue without these, using connector contracts and test data. They are required before production acceptance:

1. Temporary read-only Bayzat admin access, or a complete screen recording/export of every enabled page, setting, policy and report.
2. Bayzat exports: employees, dependants, documents, leave policies/balances/history, attendance/shifts, payroll components and 24 months of runs/payslips/SIF files, expenses/loans, assets, applicants, performance, surveys, workflows, tickets and insurance census.
3. Approved UAE HR policies and named HR/payroll signatory for calculation examples.
4. Current provider list and contracts: bank/exchange/WPS, corporate cards, insurance broker/carrier, accounting, biometrics and calendar/email.
5. Five anonymised acceptance examples for leave, attendance, payroll, EOS, expense and insurance scenarios.
6. Dubai testers: one employee, one line manager, HR, payroll/finance and director, with a 60-minute slot at each 15-day checkpoint.

## Current implementation status

- Live foundation: Supabase/Postgres, Google SSO, employee roster, RBAC/RLS, immutable audit, jobs, notifications, storage/DAM, Xero/Google connector patterns, tickets, task board, dashboards shell and production CI checks.
- Building now: durable leave/attendance, Core HR/ESS, assets/onboarding/letters, ATS/performance/surveys, and security hardening around legacy HR/payroll routes.
- Next build batch: shift/timesheet plus UAE payroll/expenses/loans. These have the highest operational and cutover risk.
- Provider-dependent: banking/cards/FX, insurance brokerage, WPS submission, biometrics and health/wellness services.
